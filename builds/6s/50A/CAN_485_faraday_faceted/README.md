## 1. The result that makes this build possible

`docs/tools/hinge_placement.py` cuts an **existing layout** along X and reports
what crosses. On the flat board every such cut carries 3 Power-netclass
conductors, because the power stage spans 26.96 mm in X and the logic spans
28.74 mm, overlapping over 26.96 mm — so every cut slices the FET bridge.

That is a property of the layout, not of faceting. Partitioning by **function**
instead — the whole power stage on one panel, logic and comms on the other —
gives a different answer, measured by
[`kicad/tools/partition_panels.py`](kicad/tools/partition_panels.py):

```text
interconnect: 19 nets
  Default   18   3V3, ADC_IU/IV/IW, GD_SPI_*, PWM_A/B/C H+L,
                 U5 ENABLE, U5 nFAULT, VBUS_SENSE, VREF_MID
  Power      1   GND  -- L-side is C10,C2,C3,C4,J1,R10,U1,U2,U3,U4:
                        decoupling and logic only, no power-stage part
  Isolated   0

VERDICT: SIGNAL-ONLY interconnect
```

**No 50 A crosses the joint.** The pack current runs
`J5A → Q1/Q3/Q5 drains → phases → J4A/B/C`, and returns
`shunts R1–R3 → J5B`, entirely inside Panel P.

**This reverses the earlier conclusion.** A geometric cut forced
`Faceted separate boards` because a flex hinge cannot pass 50 A. With the
functional partition the joint is signal-only, so **both faceted options are
viable again** and the choice reverts to the remaining discriminator — the
flex-standard acquisition gate (`TODO.md` 16.2), which only `Faceted
rigid-flex` must clear.

**No isolated net crosses either.** Both transceivers sit on Panel L, so the
isolation barrier stays wholly on one panel and never reaches the joint. That
sidesteps the constraint that no facet can satisfy the 31.86 mm
`isolation_envelope.py` minimum.

## 2. Panel assignment

Seeded by function, then propagated by net majority to a fixed point, so a
decoupling capacitor follows the device it decouples.

### Panel P — power stage (30 parts)

`Q1`–`Q6`, `U5`, `U6`–`U8`, `R1`–`R3`, `J4A/B/C`, `J5A/B`, `SH1`, plus
`C1, C5–C9, R5, R8` and `FID1`–`FID3`.

**`U6`–`U8` (current-sense amplifiers) are on the power panel deliberately.**
Their inputs are the small differential shunt signals. Keeping each amplifier
beside its shunt means the joint carries the amplified single-ended `ADC_Ix`
output instead of a noise-sensitive differential pair — which is the entire
reason a sense amplifier sits near its sense element.

### Panel L — logic and comms (35 parts)

`U1` (MCU), `U2` (secure element), `U3` (isolated CAN-FD), `U4` (isolated
RS-485), `J1`–`J3`, `FB1`–`FB4`, logic passives, `FID4`–`FID6`.

## 3. Panel envelopes

| | Panel P | Panel L |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parts | 30 | 35 |
| Courtyard area | 1324.7 mm² | 742.8 mm² |
| Widest part | 22.75 mm (`SH1`) | 13.48 mm (`U4`) |
| Fits 23.98 mm strip? | **OK**, 1.23 mm spare | **OK**, 10.50 mm spare |

**The area figures over-count and must not be used as a length budget.**
They sum courtyard bounding boxes, so every part underneath `SH1` is counted
twice — once for itself and once inside the can's 390 mm² footprint. A real
length follows from placement, not from this sum.

**`SH1` is the binding width constraint on Panel P:** 22.75 mm against a
23.98 mm strip leaves 0.6 mm per edge, which is not a workable board margin.
Either the Faraday can is re-selected for this form factor, or per-panel
shielding replaces it. Tracked as `TODO.md` 16.4e.

## 4. Interconnect

19 conductors — a 20-way board-to-board connector, or a 20-conductor flex.

| Group | Count | Nets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate drive | 6 | `PWM_AH/AL/BH/BL/CH/CL` |
| Gate-driver SPI | 4 | `GD_SPI_MISO/MOSI/SCK/CS` |
| Gate-driver control | 2 | `U5 ENABLE`, `U5 nFAULT` |
| Current sense (amplified) | 3 | `ADC_IU/IV/IW` |
| Analog reference | 1 | `VREF_MID` |
| Pack voltage sense | 1 | `VBUS_SENSE` |
| Supply | 2 | `3V3`, `GND` |

**Open:** `VREF_MID` crosses the joint as the sense amplifiers' analog
reference. Generating it locally on Panel P instead would remove a
noise-sensitive analog net from the interconnect. Not yet decided.

## 5. What is not done

- **Placement.** No parts are placed and no board files exist. Producing a
  valid placement inside a 23.98 mm strip is layout work with real DFM and
  thermal consequences; inventing one would be worse than leaving it open.
  `TODO.md` 16.5.
- **Panel P length.** Follows from placement. The FET bridge must go from its
  3×2 arrangement (26.96 mm across) to 2×3 to fit the strip.
- **Routing, gerbers, stackup** (`TODO.md` 15.2), **mounting scheme**
  (`TODO.md` 16.4h — this is what decides vibration survival, and both panels
  must be independently mounted so neither is cantilevered off the other).
- **Host envelope confirmation** (`TODO.md` 16.3) — R = 30 mm and the 2.5 mm
  depth budget are the owner's figures; the nacelle's internal length has not
  been checked against either panel.
- **Interconnect part selection** — no connector, busbar or flex is chosen.

## 6. Reproducing the partition

```bash
python3 kicad/tools/partition_panels.py \
    --board ../CAN_485_faraday_sameend/kicad/open_secure_esc_6s_50a_can485_faraday_sameend.kicad_pcb \
    --json kicad/partition.json
```

KiCad **9.0.2** only. The parent board is `version 20241229` /
`generator_version "9.0"` and must not be advanced to KiCad 10.
